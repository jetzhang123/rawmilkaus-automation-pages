RawMilkAUS Automation

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RawMilkAUS Automation accesses, processes, stores, and handles information obtained through Google APIs.

RawMilkAUS Automation is a private desktop-based operational tool used by a single authorised operator to support customer service and administrative tasks.

1. Information We Access

With Google account authorisation, the application accesses Google Sheets data required for customer support operations.

This information may include:

  • Customer names and contact information.
  • Customer account and subscription information.
  • Customer service notes and enquiry records.
  • Other information contained in authorised spreadsheets.

The application may also access the authentication information necessary to maintain an authorised connection to Google APIs.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google Sheets is used for legitimate customer support and administrative purposes, including:

  • Locating relevant customer records.
  • Matching customer enquiries to existing records.
  • Reviewing customer account information.
  • Preparing and updating customer support notes.
  • Maintaining operational records and task queues.

Google user data is not accessed for advertising, marketing profiling, or sale to data brokers.

3. Local Data Storage

The application operates primarily on the authorised operator's local computer.

Customer information may be temporarily or persistently stored in local operational files, including customer support queues, processing records, and related administrative documents.

OAuth authentication credentials may also be stored locally to maintain authorised Google API access.

4. AI-Assisted Processing

The application may be used alongside AI-assisted tools to support customer record matching, information interpretation, and preparation of administrative notes.

Where an external AI service is used, information included in a processing request may be transmitted to that service for the purpose of completing the requested operational task.

The identity of the AI service provider and its processing arrangements depend on the configuration of the AI tools in use.

External AI processing should be limited to information necessary for the relevant task and must be consistent with applicable authorisations and data protection requirements.

5. Data Sharing

Access to the desktop application is restricted to its authorised operator.

Customer information may be processed through Google services and, where enabled, external AI services supporting the application's functions.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for targeted advertising.

6. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are intended to protect locally stored information and authentication credentials from unauthorised access.

Google API access is controlled through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorisation mechanism.

Access to customer information should be limited to authorised operational purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored operational information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support customer service, record-keeping, and related administrative activities.

Local records may be reviewed and deleted when they are no longer required, subject to applicable business and legal retention obligations.

Requests concerning access to or deletion of locally held application data can be directed to the contact email below.

8. Google Account Permissions

Google API access requires explicit authorisation from the relevant Google account.

Authorisation can be revoked through the Google Account permissions settings.

Revoking authorisation prevents further access using the revoked permission but does not automatically delete information previously stored in local operational files.

9. Google API Limited Use

The application's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google user data must only be used or transferred for permitted purposes consistent with those requirements and the functionality described in this policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality, data handling practices, or applicable requirements.

Material changes to the handling of Google user data will require appropriate notice and consent where applicable.
