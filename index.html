Customer Support Automation

RawMilkAUS Automation is a private operational application designed to support authorised customer service and administrative workflows.

The application integrates with Google Sheets to help authorised operators retrieve customer records, identify relevant information, and maintain customer support notes.

What the Application Does

  • Searches authorised Google Sheets records to assist with customer enquiries.
  • Helps identify relevant customer records using available identifying information.
  • Supports the preparation and updating of customer service notes.
  • Assists with administrative record-keeping and customer support workflows.

Access and Authorisation

This application is intended for authorised operational use only. Access to Google Sheets requires explicit Google account authorisation.

Users can review and revoke Google account permissions through their Google Account settings.

Privacy and Data Protection

Information accessed through Google APIs is used to support the application's operational functions. For further information, please review our Privacy Policy.
